LENTIL, CHICKPEA, AND CABBAGE SOUP

Ingridients: lentil (2 handful), 1 can of pre-cooked chickpea, 1/2 cabbage, 1L of chicken stock, 1 can of tomato paste, veggetables of your choice 
Optional: celery, bayleaf and parsley

1. Boil 1L water and 1 tbs salt. Add 2 handful of lentil. Boil for 25min. Drain the lentil. (discard the water)
2. Open 1 can of chickpea - wash and drain. 
3. Chop or slice half a cabbage.
4. Slice a whole onion. (optional)
5. Slice 2 stalks of celery (optional)
6. Boil 1L unsalted chicken stock. Add 1 can of tomato paste.
7. Add all prepared veggies and beans. Boil 25 min in medium heat. 

Optional spices.
Parsley, bay leaf
